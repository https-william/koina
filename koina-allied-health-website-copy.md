## 2. About Page

### Our Story
Koina Allied Health started from a gap we kept seeing: services built around a funding category first and a person second. We wanted to flip that — build a practice around good clinical care, then make sure it works cleanly within NDIS, Aged Care, and DVA systems, rather than the other way around.

The name Koina points to connection and shared purpose — the idea that care works best when it's a partnership, not something done *to* someone.

### Our Approach
We don't run a one-size-fits-all program. A veteran managing chronic pain, a participant building daily living skills, and an older client wanting to stay independent at home all need different things from us — so our plans are built session by session, not off a template.

### Our Values
- **Reliability** — we show up, and we follow through.
- **Clarity** — plain language, honest timelines, no fine print surprises.
- **Respect** — for your background, your pace, and your right to decide.
- **Craft** — our clinicians keep their skills current and their standards high.

#### Occupational Therapy
Practical support for daily living — home modifications, equipment and assistive technology assessments, and functional capacity building for people of all ages.

#### Physiotherapy
Treatment for mobility, pain, falls risk, and recovery after injury, surgery, or a hospital stay — including exercise programs designed to be realistic, not aspirational.

#### Speech Pathology
Support for communication and swallowing difficulties, including AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) where needed.

#### Psychology & Counselling
A confidential space to work through what's difficult — grief, transition, trauma, anxiety, or the everyday weight of managing a health condition.

#### Support Coordination & Care Navigation
Help understanding your plan or package and connecting with the right providers — framed for NDIS participants as Support Coordination, and for Aged Care or DVA clients as Care Navigation.

## 4. Funding & Eligibility Page

### However You're Funded, We Can Help

Koina Allied Health works across three funding pathways, plus private clients — so you don't need to shop around for a provider that understands your situation.

**NDIS**
We support plan-managed, self-managed, and agency-managed participants (where registered). Not sure what your plan covers? We'll help you work it out.

**Aged Care**
We work with clients on a Home Care Package or accessing the Commonwealth Home Support Programme (CHSP), as well as people arranging their own care privately.

**DVA**
We see Gold Card and eligible White Card holders under DVA arrangements, with billing handled directly wherever possible.

**Private**
No referral, plan, or package required — just book directly.
